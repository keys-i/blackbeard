# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to this repository's issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss the change, not the person.
Corrections and different approaches are welcome when offered in good faith.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks
- sharing another person's private information without permission
- spam, advertising, or unrelated promotion
- plagiarism or misrepresenting someone else's work as your own
- posting piracy links, credentials, private tracker material, or instructions
  for bypassing access controls
- deliberately disrupting issues, reviews, releases, or project services

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour must do so immediately.

## Lawful and AI-assisted contributions

Only contribute material you are allowed to share. AI-assisted contributions
are permitted, but the contributor remains responsible for accuracy,
licensing, privacy, security, and originality. Do not submit generated work
that you cannot explain or verify.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, `@keys-i`, where
possible. Do not include personal or sensitive details in a public issue.
GitHub reporting channels may be more appropriate for serious matters.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its
contributors.
